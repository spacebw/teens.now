COPPA law

COPPA (officially Children Online Privacy Protection Act, but in reality a law that ruined internet), getting in power in 1998, bans collecting data of teens under 13 w/o verified parents' (!) consent.

But most sites realized its better just to ban everyone under 13. It's crazy, but it's just start)

New COPPA 2.0 that as of oct 4 2026 is already in Congress law extends ban on ppl under 17, creates an eraser btn (meaning parents now can erase teen's work and the site don't have right to create recover windows) and - the main badness - closed the age lying loophole.

The irony that a law that originally was made to protect privacy actually forced websites to collect behavorial data to ensure it's adult..
